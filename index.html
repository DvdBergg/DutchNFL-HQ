League rules

Prizes, Beat the Median, Guillotine, draft and dues
Champion and runner-up$220 + shirt · $100

Decided through the fantasy playoffs at the end of the regular season.

Beat the Median$100

Each week you play your matchup and the league median. Beat the median for an extra win, fall below it for an extra loss. Win your matchup and beat the median: 2-0. Lose the matchup but beat the median: 1-1. Best record at the end of the regular season wins.

Guillotine$50

The lowest-scoring team each week is eliminated from the Guillotine. You keep playing in the league for the championship. The last team remaining wins.

Highest weekly team score$50

The single biggest team score of the regular season. Only one week matters.

Highest individual player score$50

The manager whose player posts the highest fantasy total in a single game wins. Example: Ja'Marr Chase scores 47.8 in Week 9 and nobody beats it all season.

Draft and dues$50 each · all 12 paid

Draft was August 1 at 8:00 AM EST (14:00 CET) with a 10-hour clock. Every manager has paid the $50 entry.
